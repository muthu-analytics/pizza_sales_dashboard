# 🍕 Pizza Sales Data Analysis Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes a pizza sales dataset to uncover business insights and provide actionable recommendations. The goal is to help the business improve revenue, optimize operations, and make data-driven decisions.

---

## ❗ Problem Statement

The business lacks clear visibility into:

* Top-performing and underperforming products
* Customer ordering patterns
* Peak sales hours and demand trends
* Revenue contribution by category and size

As a result, decision-making is inefficient and opportunities for growth are missed.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Identify best and worst selling pizzas
* Analyze daily and hourly sales trends
* Understand customer behavior
* Evaluate sales by category and size
* Provide business recommendations

---

## 📊 Dataset Description

The dataset includes:

* `order_id` – Unique order identifier
* `order_date` – Date of order
* `order_time` – Time of order
* `pizza_name` – Pizza name
* `pizza_category` – Category (Classic, Supreme, Chicken, Veggie)
* `pizza_size` – Size (S, M, L, XL, XXL)
* `quantity` – Quantity sold
* `total_price` – Total order value

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* **Total Revenue:** 817.86K
* **Average Order Value:** 38.31
* **Total Pizzas Sold:** 50K
* **Total Orders:** 21K
* **Average Pizzas per Order:** 2.32

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 🗓️ Sales Trends

* Orders peak on **Fridays and Saturdays**
* Highest activity during **12–1 PM and 5–8 PM**

### 🍕 Product Performance

* **Top Sellers:** Classic Deluxe, BBQ Chicken, Hawaiian Pizza
* **Lowest Seller:** Brie Carre Pizza

### 📦 Category & Size Insights

* Classic category contributes the most revenue
* Large-size pizzas generate maximum sales

---

## ⚠️ Problems Identified

* Some pizzas have **very low demand** (menu inefficiency)
* Sales are **low on weekdays**
* Peak hours may cause **operational bottlenecks**
* Certain categories are **underperforming**
* Smaller pizza sizes contribute less to revenue

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* **Menu Optimization:** Remove or improve low-selling pizzas
* **Weekday Promotions:** Discounts to boost low-demand days
* **Peak Hour Management:** Allocate more staff during busy times
* **Category Marketing:** Promote underperforming categories
* **Pricing Strategy:** Focus on high-performing sizes (Large pizzas)
